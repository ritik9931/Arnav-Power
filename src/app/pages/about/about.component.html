Our Foundation

Bridging High-Precision Cartography with Sustainable Power Infrastructure

Headquartered in Wadegaon, Sadak Arjuni (Dist. Gondia) with engineering branches in Nagpur, Arnav Power Solution & Services was founded with a singular mission: to provide Indian public sector enterprises, industrial corporations, and infrastructure developers with high-reliability geospatial intelligence and clean solar energy solutions.

We operate across two core verticals that reinforce each other. Our GIS division provides the millimeter terrain mapping and 3D shadow analysis necessary to design optimum solar energy arrays, while our solar EPC division brings structural and electrical engineering capability to utility-scale projects.
